[b]Click the favorite-color square on a pawn's Bio tab to pick a new favorite color.[/b]

I used to do this in Character Editor, but it's a lot of clicking for something I do all the time. With [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2562859859]Self Dyeing[/url], which dyes apparel from a pawn's favorite color, I can now color-code the colony in a few clicks.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]The favorite-color square next to a pawn's ideoligion on the Bio tab becomes a button that opens a color picker.
[*]Works on Bio Tab+ as well as the vanilla Bio tab.
[*]Babies don't have the square in vanilla, so there's nothing to click for them.
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
Supports Bio Tab+.
Safe to add to an existing save.
